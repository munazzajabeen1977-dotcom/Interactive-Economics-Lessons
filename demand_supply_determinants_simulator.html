Determinants of Demand

Net demand shift: 0
Higher income → buyers can afford more (normal good)
Pricier substitutes push buyers here; pricier complements push them away
More buyers in the market → more demand at every price
A stronger preference for the good → buyers want more of it at every price
Expecting higher prices or income ahead → buy more now

Determinants of Supply

Net supply shift: 0
Pricier raw materials, labor, or energy → costlier to produce, less supplied
If other goods pay better, producers switch away → less supplied here
More firms in the market → more supplied at every price
Better technology or methods → cheaper to produce more
Expecting higher prices ahead → hold back stock now, supply less today
How to read it: each slider is one determinant working alone — the curve shift you see is the sum of everything set on that side. Demand determinants move the yellow curve; supply determinants move the coral curve. Where the two curves cross is the equilibrium: the price and quantity where the market clears.
