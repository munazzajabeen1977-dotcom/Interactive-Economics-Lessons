Eḏ  =  % Δ Quantity Demanded% Δ Price general definition
The price at which quantity demanded falls to zero — where the curve meets the price axis
Move along the demand curve — low price toward the quantity axis, high price toward the price axis
Changes how the curve looks, but not its elasticity at a given price — try it and watch Eḏ stay put
Eḏ = 0Perfectly inelastic — quantity doesn't respond at all
0 < Eḏ < 1Inelastic — quantity responds less than price
Eḏ = 1Unit elastic — quantity responds proportionally
Eḏ > 1Elastic — quantity responds more than price
Eḏ = ∞Perfectly elastic — any price rise drops quantity to zero
How to read it: on any straight demand line, the top half (near the price-axis intercept) is elastic, the exact midpoint is unit elastic, and the bottom half (near the quantity-axis) is inelastic. Where demand is elastic, raising price lowers revenue; where it's inelastic, raising price raises revenue — that's why the revenue rectangle grows and shrinks as you slide.
