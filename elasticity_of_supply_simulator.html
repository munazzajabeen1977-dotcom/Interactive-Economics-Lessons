Eₛ  =  % Δ Quantity Supplied% Δ Price general definition
Negative → curve crosses the quantity axis (inelastic). Zero → passes through the origin (unit elastic). Positive → crosses the price axis (elastic).
Move along the current supply curve
Changes how the curve looks, but not its elasticity — try it and watch Eₛ stay put
Eₛ = 0Perfectly inelastic — a vertical curve, fixed quantity regardless of price
0 < Eₛ < 1Inelastic — curve crosses the quantity axis
Eₛ = 1Unit elastic — curve passes through the origin
Eₛ > 1Elastic — curve crosses the price axis
Eₛ = ∞Perfectly elastic — a horizontal curve at the price-axis intercept
How to read it: a straight-line supply curve is elastic everywhere if it crosses the price axis, inelastic everywhere if it crosses the quantity axis, and exactly unit elastic everywhere if it passes through the origin — try dragging the price slider at a fixed intercept and watch the category stay the same even as the exact number moves.
