Perfect competition
The firm is a price taker — it can sell any quantity at this price, so Price = MR
Monopoly demand curve
How to read it: the firm keeps producing as long as another unit adds more to revenue than to cost — so it stops exactly where MR = MC. Profit is the shaded rectangle between price and average cost at that quantity (green if price exceeds AC, red if it doesn't). In perfect competition, price is fixed by the market, so MR is a flat line. In monopoly, selling more requires cutting price on every unit, so MR falls faster than demand and sits below it.
