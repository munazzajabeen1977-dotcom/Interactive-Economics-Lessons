How to read it: the budget line shows every affordable combination of X and Y. Each indifference curve traces bundles giving equal satisfaction — curves further from the origin mean more utility. The consumer does best at the point where an indifference curve is exactly tangent to the budget line — that's the highest utility they can reach without overspending, and it's where the slope of the indifference curve (MRS) equals the slope of the budget line (the price ratio).
