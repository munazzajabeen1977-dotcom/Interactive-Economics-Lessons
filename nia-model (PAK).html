Statistical discrepancy:
National Income = compensation + rents + interest + proprietors'/mixed income + corporate profits. Adding back net indirect taxes, then depreciation, then subtracting net factor income from abroad (dominated here by workers' remittances) reconstructs GDP from the income side, independently of the expenditure side.
