Statistical discrepancy:
National Income = compensation + rents + interest + proprietors' income + corporate profits. Adding back net indirect taxes, then depreciation, then subtracting net factor income from abroad reconstructs GDP from the income side — independently of the expenditure side. Real statistical agencies report the gap between the two as the statistical discrepancy.
