The fixed recipe — e.g. 1 left shoe needs exactly this many right shoes
How to read it: the indifference curves are L-shaped, kinked exactly along a ray from the origin where X/α = Y/β. Since more of only one good adds no utility without the other, the optimal bundle always sits on that ray — try changing prices and watch the point slide along it rather than substituting toward the cheaper good. With perfect complements there's no substitution effect at all; a price change only produces an income effect.
