How to read it: because the indifference curves are straight lines, they either lie flatter or steeper than the budget line — never tangent to it at an interior point. The consumer compares utility per dollar on each good (a÷Px vs b÷Py) and spends everything on whichever is higher — a corner solution. Only when the two ratios are exactly equal is the consumer willing to buy any mix, because the indifference line and the budget line coincide.
