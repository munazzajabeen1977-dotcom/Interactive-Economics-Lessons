How to read it: growth and employment push the whole frontier outward in a parallel shift — the economy can make more of everything, but the trade-off between the two goods at any given point looks similar in shape. Technology aimed at just one good produces a biased shift — the frontier stretches further along that axis than the other, changing the trade-off itself, not just the scale of it.
