The curve is a production possibilities frontier: it bows outward because resources aren't equally suited to producing both goods. Choice means picking a point along it; scarcity means you can never get outside it; opportunity cost is what the curve's slope tells you — how much of one good you give up to get more of the other. Notice the cost climbs as you push toward either end, since the last resources you shift over are the ones least suited to the new job.
