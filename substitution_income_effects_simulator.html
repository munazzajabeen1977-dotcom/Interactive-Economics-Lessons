How to read it: the substitution effect is the move from the original bundle to a hypothetical point on the same indifference curve, priced at the new ratio (the dashed compensated line) — it isolates the pure effect of X becoming relatively cheaper or dearer, with satisfaction held constant. The income effect is the rest of the move, from that hypothetical point to the actual new bundle on the new budget line — it captures the change in real purchasing power. The two always add up to the total change in X.
