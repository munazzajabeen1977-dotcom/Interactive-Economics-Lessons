How to read it: switch the toggle between "levied on sellers" and "levied on buyers" — notice the price buyers pay, the price sellers keep, the quantity traded, and the burden split all stay exactly the same. The economic incidence of a tax depends on relative elasticity, not on who is legally required to send the money to the government. The flatter (more elastic) side of the market always bears the smaller share of the tax.
